Who we are

Building a more sustainable world, one project at a time

Engineers for a Sustainable World (ESW) is an international non-profit network of students and professionals united by a shared passion for technical sustainability. Our University of New Brunswick chapter is part of that network — more than 50 chapters across North America.

As a chapter, we design and build real solutions to local and global sustainability problems, learn practical engineering skills outside the classroom, and bring together people who want to make an impact. Whether you're an engineer, scientist, designer, or just curious, there's a place for you here.

What drives us

The way we work

Purposeful

Our work is constructive and impactful. Being part of ESW means contributing something real to our community.

Dynamic

We're a creative, agile group that tries new things and gives members eye-opening, hands-on experiences.

Community

We work, learn, and have fun together — in our chapter, on campus, and across the wider ESW network.

Optimistic
